Our Experiences

Seven Unforgettable Tours

Each tour is a doorway into a different world — from ancient pigeon towers to recycling workshops where trash becomes treasure.

🕊️

Pigeon Towers Tour

Climb the iconic pigeon towers unique to Manshyat Nasser. Learn about the beloved tradition of pigeon breeding, the different breeds, and the stunning architecture of towers that house 1,000–3,000 pigeons each.

  • Al-Safir Tower
  • 1,000–3,000 pigeons
  • Architecture & history
♻️

Recycling Workshops Tour

Visit 5–6 specialized recycling workshops, each mastering a different material — plastic, glass, cardboard, aluminum, and paper. Witness the full chain from collection to finished product.

  • 6 workshops
  • Full recycling chain
  • Local craftsmanship
🌿

Environmental Protection Association

Operating for ~40 years, this pioneering association creates handmade crafts from recycled materials and runs vital community programs including health initiatives, literacy classes, and children's education.

  • ~40 years of service
  • Handmade crafts
  • Social impact programs
⛪

Monastery of St. Simon

Established ~60 years ago, this is one of the largest monasteries in Egypt by congregation. Marvel at stunning architecture carved into the Mokattam hills — one of the most beautiful monasteries in the world.

  • ~60 years old
  • Cave churches
  • Spiritual heritage
🏛️

City of the Dead Tour

Explore the unique culture of Cairo's cemetery district. Visit Sultan Barquq Mosque, Qaitbay Mosque, and meet local craftsmen — glass makers, earring makers, and jewelry artisans.

  • Sultan Barquq Mosque
  • Qaitbay Mosque
  • Local craftsmen
🏰

Saladin Citadel

Discover the full history of the Citadel — when and why it was built, its strategic importance in Egyptian history, and the breathtaking Islamic architecture of the Mosque of Muhammad Ali within its walls.

  • Full citadel history
  • Strategic importance
  • Islamic architecture
🕌

Religious Complex in Old Cairo

Visit the historic Religious Complex — a symbol of coexistence where churches stand alongside mosques. Learn about the complex's founding, its beautiful churches, and the centuries of religious harmony in this sacred space.

  • Religious harmony
  • Historic churches
  • Cultural coexistence
